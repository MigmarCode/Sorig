About us

Sorig was founded to address a critical gap in healthcare where increasing staffing demands place strain on hospitals, and nurses are often left without the level of support needed to perform at their best.

With combined experience at the bedside, we have witnessed both the operational pressures within healthcare facilities and the challenges nurses face when navigating travel roles through traditional agencies. These experiences shaped a clear vision: to create a model that better serves both.

Sorig brings a balanced, thoughtful approach to healthcare staffing delivering reliable, high-quality professionals to healthcare facilities, while ensuring nurses are guided, supported, and valued throughout every stage of their journey.

At its core, Sorig is grounded in a shared commitment to strengthening healthcare teams and enabling the consistent delivery of exceptional patient care.

The Values We Live By

These core principles guide every placement, every partnership, and every decision we make.

Integrity

We believe in complete transparency and ethical recruitment. Our word is our bond, and our reputation is built on trust.

Compassion

Healthcare is personal. We treat every nurse and every client with the same care and respect we would show our own family.

Excellence

We don't just fill positions; we build careers. We strive for excellence in every interaction and every placement we facilitate.
